Privacy Policy for Blocky

Last updated: 16/08/2026

This privacy policy explains how Blocky ("the app") collects, uses, and shares information.

Information We Collect

Leaderboard data: If you choose to submit a score to the leaderboard, we collect the name you enter and your score. This is optional — you can skip entering a name after each game.

Advertising data: Blocky displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, general location (such as country or region), and other data to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly.

How We Use Information

  • Leaderboard names and scores are displayed publicly within the app and on 247esports.in to show top players.
  • Advertising data is used by Google AdMob to show ads and measure their performance.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying ads. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Firestore) — for storing leaderboard scores. firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Children's Privacy

Blocky is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Deletion

If you'd like a leaderboard entry removed, contact us at bekk306@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact
